\section{Limitations}

Two foundation models of the four we attempted, for the reasons given in
% [UNSCOPE] "two datasets" -> three once results_n54.tex lands.
Section~\ref{sec:design}. Two datasets, one of which has three electrodes and
is therefore a harder rather than neutral setting for encoders pretrained on
dense montages. Three seeds on the decisive comparison and one elsewhere;
frozen-probe and LaBraM arms are single-seed. Nine subjects, at which the
smallest attainable two-sided Wilcoxon $p$ is $0.0039$ --- several results here
sit at that floor, and the design is consequently well powered to detect a
large deficit and poorly powered to certify a small improvement. The
matched-input control searched two architectural hyperparameters per subject,
so the supervised broadband arm is a lower bound on what a fully tuned
comparator would achieve, and a stronger comparator would widen the deficit
rather than narrow it.

\section*{Reproducibility}
Code, per-subject result artifacts, per-trial probability files, and a
manifest with SHA256 hashes and pinned library versions accompany this paper.
Every table is regenerated from those artifacts by a single script; no number
here was transcribed by hand.
